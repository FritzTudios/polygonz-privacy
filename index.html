Privacy Policy for PolygonZ

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This privacy policy explains what information the Android app PolygonZ (published by FritzTudios) collects, why, and what choices you have. We keep it limited to what the app actually does, and we update it when that changes.

Summary

  • PolygonZ doesn't show ads and doesn't sell in-app purchases.
  • There is no PolygonZ account. We don't ask for your name, email address or contacts.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, so we can improve it.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

Product analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

PolygonZ uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure how the app is used. It can collect:

  • An app-instance identifier, and the Android advertising ID where your device makes it available
  • Basic device and app information, such as device model, Android version, app version and language
  • Approximate location (country or region), which Google derives from your IP address
  • Events about how the app is used, such as opening the app or starting and finishing a puzzle

We use this only to understand engagement and improve the game. We don't use it to show you ads or to identify you personally. Google processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google Play Games

If you're signed in to Google Play Games on your device, PolygonZ reads your Play Games player ID and display name to show your player profile in the app. We don't send them to any server of ours. Google Play Games is provided by Google under Google's privacy policy, and you can manage it in the Play Games app or your Google Account settings.

Advertising

PolygonZ currently shows no ads. The app still includes Google's mobile ads software development kit (SDK), but it isn't started, so it doesn't request or show ads. If we add ads in the future, we'll update this policy before they appear.

Data stored on your device

PolygonZ stores your game data on your device: puzzle progress, stars, coins, hints, streaks and settings. If you've turned on Android backup for your Google Account, Android may include this data in your device backup so it can be restored on a new phone. Device backup is controlled by you and handled by Google.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, PolygonZ can show reminders, such as for the daily puzzle. They're scheduled on your device; no information is sent to us to do this. You can turn them off at any time in Android settings.

Your choices

  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (for example, Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Notifications and backup: you can turn these off in Android settings.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live, you may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawing consent, and you may be able to contact a data protection authority. To exercise them, contact us at the address below.

Children's privacy

PolygonZ is not directed to children under 13. The app follows the audience settings configured in Google Play and does not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own purposes.

Data deletion

To remove the game data stored on your device, uninstall PolygonZ or clear the app's storage in Android settings.

To request deletion of data associated with your use of PolygonZ that we can reasonably help with, email fritztudios@gmail.com with the subject line PolygonZ data deletion request (requests with the earlier subject "Polygonz data deletion request" are also accepted). Include enough detail for us to understand the request, such as approximate dates of use. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Analytics data held by Google is also subject to Google's deletion and retention practices for Google Analytics for Firebase.

Changes

We'll update this policy when the app's data practices change, for example before adding ads or purchases. The "Last updated" date at the top changes when we do.
