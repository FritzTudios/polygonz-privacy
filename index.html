Privacy Policy for Polygonz

Last updated: July 13, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android app Polygonz (published by FritzTudios) uses Google advertising and analytics services. We aim to keep this description accurate and limited to what the app actually does. It may be updated when those practices change.

What we use Google services for

Polygonz uses two Google products on Android:

  • Google AdMob — to show interstitial and rewarded ads.
  • Google Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase / GA4) — to measure app usage (for example, opens and in-app events such as ad impressions) so we can understand engagement and improve the product.

Polygonz does not sell your personal information. Data handled by AdMob and Firebase Analytics is processed by Google under Google’s terms and privacy policies for those products.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Advertising (AdMob)

AdMob may collect information from your device in connection with serving and measuring ads, which can include:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Approximate location (inferred from network information)
  • App activity related to ads (such as impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostic information used for fraud prevention, security, and service quality

That information may be used for ad delivery, ad personalization (where allowed), measurement, analytics related to ads, and detecting invalid traffic. AdMob may share data with advertising partners as described by Google.

Product analytics (Firebase Analytics)

Firebase Analytics may collect app usage information, which can include identifiers associated with your app install/device, basic app and device information, and events about how the app is used (for example, that an ad was shown). We use this to measure engagement and monetization performance. We do not use Firebase Analytics to collect your email address or name.

Data stored on your device

Polygonz also stores game-related data locally on your device (such as progress and settings). That local data stays on the device unless you clear app storage or uninstall the app.

Personalized vs. non-personalized ads

Depending on your region and device settings, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. You can limit personalized advertising in your device settings or via Google’s ad controls:

https://www.google.com/settings/ads

Consent for ads (where required)

In regions where consent is required for personalized advertising, Polygonz uses a consent flow for ads. If consent for personalized ads is not granted, non-personalized ads may still be shown where allowed.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawing consent, and you may be able to contact a data protection authority.

For data processed by AdMob or Firebase Analytics, Google’s controls and policies also apply. You can start with Google’s partner-sites page linked above and your device / Google account privacy settings.

Children’s privacy

Polygonz is not directed to children under 13. The app follows the audience settings configured in Google Play and does not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own purposes.

Data deletion

To remove local game data from your device, uninstall Polygonz or clear the app’s storage in Android settings.

To request deletion of data associated with your use of Polygonz that we can reasonably help with, email fritztudios@gmail.com with the subject line Polygonz data deletion request. Include enough detail for us to understand the request (for example, the app name and approximate dates of use). We will respond within a reasonable time.

Data held by Google through AdMob or Firebase Analytics is also subject to Google’s deletion and retention practices for those services.

Changes

We may update this policy when our advertising or analytics practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when we do.
