Privacy Policy for Polygonz

Last updated: July 2026

This privacy policy explains how Polygonz handles data in connection with advertising and third‑party services. Polygonz displays interstitial and rewarded advertisements using Google AdMob. This policy is designed to remain valid as privacy regulations evolve and may be updated periodically.

Advertising and Data Collection

Polygonz uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may automatically collect certain information from your device, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Approximate location (inferred from network information)
  • App activity related to ad interactions (such as impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostic information used for fraud prevention, security, and service improvement

This information may be used for purposes such as ad personalization, analytics, ad measurement, and detecting invalid traffic. AdMob may share aggregated or pseudonymized data with its advertising partners as described in Google’s Privacy & Terms.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Regional Compliance (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, and Future Regulations)

Polygonz aims to comply with major global privacy regulations. As laws evolve, this policy may be updated to reflect new requirements.

Legal Bases for Processing (EEA/UK)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, personal data processed through AdMob may rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — for personalized advertising.
  • Legitimate interests — for non‑personalized ads, fraud prevention, security, and basic ad delivery.
  • Compliance with legal obligations — when required by applicable law.

California (CCPA/CPRA)

California users may have rights related to accessing, deleting, or opting out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. Polygonz does not directly sell or share personal data. Any data handling performed by AdMob follows Google’s Privacy & Terms.

Brazil (LGPD)

Brazilian users may have rights including confirmation of processing, access, correction, deletion, portability, and information about data‑sharing practices. Requests related to AdMob‑processed data should be directed to Google.

Personalized vs. Non‑Personalized Ads

Depending on your region and device settings, you may receive personalized or non‑personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time by adjusting your device’s advertising settings or by visiting Google’s ad personalization controls:

https://www.google.com/settings/ads

Consent Management (EEA/UK and Other Regions Requiring Consent)

Polygonz uses a consent management platform (CMP) for users in regions where consent is legally required for personalized advertising. If consent is not granted, only non‑personalized ads will be shown. As additional regions adopt consent requirements, the CMP may be expanded to support those jurisdictions.

User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • Access
  • Rectification
  • Erasure
  • Restriction of processing
  • Objection
  • Data portability
  • Withdrawal of consent
  • Lodging a complaint with a supervisory authority

Requests related to data collected by AdMob should be directed to Google, as Polygonz does not collect or store this data directly.

Children’s Privacy

Polygonz is not directed toward children under 13. The app follows the target‑audience settings configured in Google Play Console and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If future regulations introduce new age‑based requirements, Polygonz will update its compliance accordingly.

Data Deletion

Polygonz does not collect or store personal data directly. Any data collected by AdMob is handled according to Google’s Privacy & Terms. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game files.

Third‑Party Links and Updates

This privacy policy may include links to third‑party services such as Google. These services may update their policies independently. Polygonz will update this privacy policy as needed to remain compliant with evolving regulations and platform requirements.
